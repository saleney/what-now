# What Now? Project Instructions

This project is a gentle screen-free activity generator intended to help families move from indecision into a simple, age-appropriate activity.

## Creative direction

- Keep the experience warm, practical, playful, and low-pressure.
- Avoid turning it into a dense parenting app or productivity tool.
- Recommendations should feel doable with ordinary materials and realistic family energy.
- Preserve the existing simple interaction loop.

## Child and family considerations

- Activities should be developmentally sensible for the selected age/group.
- Avoid unsafe suggestions, unrealistic supervision assumptions, or activities requiring obscure supplies unless clearly stated.
- Keep instructions concise enough to use in the moment.
- Favor open-ended play, movement, creativity, connection, and curiosity over passive screen activities.

## UX and mobile

- Mobile is a first-class experience because this is likely to be used in the moment on a phone.
- Keep controls comfortably tappable and results readable without awkward scrolling or repositioning.
- Prevent overflow/clipping and keep the repeat/generate-another flow obvious.

## Code and scope

- `index.html` currently contains the entire project: markup, styling, activity data, and interaction logic.
- Inspect before changing.
- Prefer the smallest clean implementation.
- Do not refactor the whole single-file site merely for architectural neatness unless explicitly requested.
- Preserve unrelated content and behavior.
- Keep agent work concise and token-efficient.

## Testing

For meaningful changes, test representative age/category selections and repeated generations on small mobile, standard mobile, large mobile, and desktop.
